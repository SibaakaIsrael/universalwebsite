Universal Welders and Fabricators Ltd is a premier metal fabrication and welding company delivering high-quality structural, mechanical, and custom steel work across industrial, commercial, and residential sectors.

Our team of certified welders and fabricators bring decades of hands-on experience to every project — from small custom brackets to large-scale structural steel frameworks — ensuring precision, safety, and durability in everything we build.
